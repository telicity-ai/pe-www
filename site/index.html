Three steps, one approval

What you tried above is a sketch of the skill. Installed, it works on your running page.

01

The agent sketches

It reads your project once and lays two to four drawn options over each part of the page that matters.

02

You point at the dots

Each option shows on the page itself, at desktop and phone width. Click to keep it. No design vocabulary.

03

It builds what you approved

The built page is measured against the approved one, and every difference is fixed or explained.

Installed, it learns your taste

The star follows your choices

Each approval is kept on your machine as pattern ids, never your page's text. Next time the suggested mark sits where you chose before: at once in the same project, elsewhere once a choice repeats.
